About the Workshop

Our workshop welcomes contributions on exploring the challenges of developing benchmark datasets for short videos, findings from solving existing video understanding problems, and explorations of novel research problems and application areas.

Topics of Interest

Data Collection Challenges

Exploring challenges in assembling high-quality, ethically sourced short video data that capture diversity in themes, contents, editing, and composition styles.

Analysis and Understanding

Establishing performance baselines by building on existing techniques for video understanding problems including action recognition, segmentation, and content captioning.

New Research Frontiers

Investigating research problems specific to short videos, including looped narrative detection, generation, and quality evaluation at scale.
